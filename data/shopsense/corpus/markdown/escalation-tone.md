# Escalation Triggers and Customer Communication Standard

**Kartway Support Interaction Escalation Policy**

1. **Purpose**: This policy outlines the triggers for escalating a support interaction from automated systems to human support agents, ensuring timely and effective resolution of complex or sensitive customer issues.

2. **Scope**: This policy applies to all support interactions initiated through Kartway's online platforms, including chat, email, and phone support.

3. **Escalation Triggers**: A support interaction must be escalated to a human support agent in the following situations:
	* 3.1: The customer explicitly requests to speak with a human support agent.
	* 3.2: The customer uses abusive language or tone, as defined in the Kartway Code of Conduct.
	* 3.3: The customer contacts support for the third time regarding the same issue, as tracked by the support ticketing system.
	* 3.4: The customer mentions potential legal action or regulatory complaint against Kartway.
	* 3.5: The customer reports any issue related to physical harm or product safety.
	* 3.6: The customer requests a refund exceeding the automated refund cap of $200.

4. **Tone Guidance**: Human support agents must respond to escalated interactions with empathy and professionalism, while maintaining a neutral tone. Agents should acknowledge the customer's concerns and provide clear explanations of Kartway's policies and procedures.

5. **Liability Apology Prohibition**: Under no circumstances should a support agent apologize in a manner that admits liability on behalf of Kartway. Agents are trained to express regret for the customer's experience without implying fault or responsibility.

6. **Exception**: In cases where a customer is contacting support regarding a critical issue, such as a product safety concern, the support agent may involve the Kartway Product Safety Team, led by Rachel Lee, to ensure prompt and effective resolution.

7. **Escalation Deadline**: Support interactions that meet the escalation criteria must be transferred to a human support agent within 2 hours of the initial contact, or by the end of the next business day if the interaction occurs outside of business hours.

8. **Monitoring and Review**: The Kartway Support Operations Team, led by Michael Patel, will regularly review escalated interactions to ensure compliance with this policy and identify areas for improvement.

9. **Revision History**: This policy was last updated on February 10, 2023. Revisions are approved by the Kartway Executive Team and communicated to all support staff.
