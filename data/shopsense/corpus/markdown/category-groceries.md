# Category Policy Addendum: Groceries

**Kartway Customer Operations Policy Addendum: Groceries**

**1. Return Window**
For Groceries, the return window is 14 days from the date of delivery, which differs from the standard 30-day return window for other categories.

**2. Condition Requirements**
To be eligible for return, Groceries must be in their original condition, with all seals and packaging intact. Specifically, the product must have at least 75% of its original weight or volume remaining, and the packaging must not be damaged or tampered with in any way. For example, a bottle of juice must have at least 75% of its original volume remaining, and the packaging must not be damaged or tampered with.

**3. Original Packaging**
For Groceries, original packaging is mandatory for returns, except for products with a value of $5 or less, such as single-serve snacks or condiments.

**4. Restocking Fees**
A restocking fee of 25% of the product's value will be applied to all returned Groceries, except for products that are defective or damaged upon arrival. However, this fee will be waived if the customer returns the product within 7 days of delivery.

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling**
Due to the perishable nature of Groceries, all returns must be handled and shipped in accordance with Kartway's Hazardous and Perishable Materials Handling Policy (KHP-001). Specifically, all returns must be shipped via expedited shipping (e.g., 2-day or overnight) to prevent spoilage, and must be packaged in a way that prevents leakage or damage during transit.

**6. Warranty Period**
The warranty period for Groceries is 60 days from the date of delivery, which applies to all products in this category, except for products with a "Best By" or "Use By" date, which are not eligible for warranty claims. During this period, customers can report any issues with their products, and Kartway will work with the seller to resolve the issue, which may include a replacement or refund, as determined by the Kartway Support Team, led by **Amanda Rodriguez**, Director of Customer Operations.

**7. Exceptions**
The following products are exempt from the above policies:
* Products sold by **FreshMart**, a trusted partner of Kartway, which has its own return and warranty policies.
* Products with a value of $20 or more, which require a return merchandise authorization (RMA) number from the Kartway Support Team before they can be returned.

By adhering to these policies, Kartway aims to provide a seamless and satisfactory shopping experience for its customers, while also ensuring the quality and safety of the products sold on its platform.
