# Category Policy Addendum: Toys

**Toys Category Policy Addendum**

**1. Return Window**
For Toys category items, the return window is 45 days from the date of delivery, exceeding the standard 30-day return window applicable to other categories on the Kartway marketplace.

**2. Condition Requirements**
Toys must be returned in their original condition, with all original parts, instructions, and accessories included. Items must have less than 10% of their components missing or damaged to be eligible for a full refund. Specifically, for puzzles, at least 90% of the pieces must be present.

**3. Original Packaging**
Original packaging is mandatory for returns of toys with a value over $50. For items valued between $20 and $50, original packaging is recommended but not required. However, a 5% restocking fee will be applied if the item is not in its original packaging.

**4. Restocking Fees**
A restocking fee of 15% will be applied to all toys returned with damage exceeding 5% of the item's components. This fee increases to 25% for items with more than 20% of their components missing or damaged.

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling**
Toys that require batteries must have them removed before return to prevent damage or fire hazard. For toys containing perishable components (e.g., play food), a special handling fee of $8.50 will be deducted from the refund if these components are not properly sealed and packaged.

**6. Warranty Period**
The warranty period for toys on the Kartway marketplace is 2 years from the date of purchase for mechanical and electronic components, provided the item has been used in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions. For non-mechanical toys, the warranty period is 1 year. Exceptions include products from the "Zylax" brand, which carry a 5-year warranty, and "Kiddio" brand toys, which have a 3-year warranty.

**7. Specific Exceptions**
- The "Lego Compatible" series by Brickzone has a return window of 60 days.
- The "Educational Electronics" line by MindSpark carries a restocking fee of 10% regardless of condition.
- All toy purchases over $200 are eligible for free return shipping if initiated within the first 30 days of the 45-day return window.

**8. Compliance and Updates**
This policy is effective as of January 1, 2023, and supersedes all previous versions. Support agents are required to familiarize themselves with these guidelines to ensure compliance with Kartway's customer satisfaction standards. Updates to this policy will be communicated through the Kartway internal portal and are binding 14 days after publication.
