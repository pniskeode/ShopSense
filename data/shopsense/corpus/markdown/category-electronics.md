# Category Policy Addendum: Electronics

**Kartway Electronics Policy Addendum**

**1. Return Window**
For Electronics products, the return window is 15 days from the date of delivery, unless otherwise specified by the seller. This deviates from the standard 30-day return window applicable to other categories on the Kartway marketplace.

**2. Condition Requirements**
Electronics items must be returned in their original condition with all original accessories, including cables, manuals, and batteries. The item must not show any signs of wear or damage, with the exception of the Kaltos brand, which allows for minor scratches on the exterior casing, provided the product functions as intended.

**3. Original Packaging**
Original packaging is mandatory for returns of Electronics items priced over $200. For items priced $200 or less, original packaging is not required but is recommended to prevent damage during transit.

**4. Restocking Fees**
A restocking fee of 12% applies to all Electronics returns, with a minimum fee of $8 and a maximum fee of $25. This fee does not apply to defective items or items that were not as described.

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling**
Electronics items that contain lithium batteries, such as laptops and tablets, must be shipped back to the seller using a Kartway-approved hazardous materials carrier. A list of approved carriers can be found on the Kartway Seller Portal. Perishable Electronics items, such as those with limited shelf life or requiring climate-controlled storage, are not allowed on the Kartway marketplace, with the exception of items from the registered seller, Zenon Electronics, which has a special exemption to sell temperature-controlled gadgets.

**6. Warranty Period**
The warranty period for Electronics items on Kartway is 2 years from the date of purchase, unless the seller specifies a longer warranty period. For items from the Xylon brand, the warranty period is 5 years. In the event of a warranty claim, the buyer must contact the seller within 45 days of discovering the issue, and the seller must respond with a remedy within 10 business days.

**7. Special Exceptions**
The following sellers are exempt from the restocking fee requirement: Nexarion, Voltara, and Kalimax. These sellers may also extend the return window to 20 days from the date of delivery, provided they clearly indicate this on their product listings.

**8. Compliance**
All sellers of Electronics items on the Kartway marketplace must comply with these policies, as well as all applicable laws and regulations, including those related to consumer protection, product safety, and environmental sustainability. Failure to comply may result in account suspension or termination, as outlined in the Kartway Seller Agreement.
