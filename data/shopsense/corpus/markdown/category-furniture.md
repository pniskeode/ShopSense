# Category Policy Addendum: Furniture

**Kartway Furniture Policy Addendum**

**1.1 Return Window**
For Furniture items, the return window is 45 days from the date of delivery, as opposed to the standard 30-day return window for other categories.

**1.2 Condition Requirements**
To be eligible for return, Furniture items must be in their original condition with no signs of assembly, usage, or damage. Specifically, items must have less than 5 miles of transportation distance logged since delivery and no more than 2 instances of minor scratches or scuffs per item.

**1.3 Original Packaging**
Original packaging is mandatory for returns of Furniture items. Sellers are required to ensure that all original packaging materials, including boxes, instructions, and hardware, are included with the returned item. In cases where original packaging is missing or damaged, a restocking fee of 12% will be applied.

**1.4 Restocking Fees**
A restocking fee of 8% will be applied to all returned Furniture items, regardless of reason. This fee will be deducted from the refund amount. However, if the return is due to a defect or error on the part of the seller, no restocking fee will be applied.

**1.5 Hazardous or Perishable Handling**
Furniture items that contain hazardous materials, such as certain types of foam or chemicals, must be handled and returned in accordance with Kartway's Hazardous Materials Policy. Perishable items, such as items with integrated plants or living materials, are not eligible for return.

**1.6 Warranty Period**
The warranty period for Furniture items is 2 years from the date of delivery, except for items from the "Kartway Premium" brand, which have a 5-year warranty period. During this time, sellers are responsible for providing replacement parts or repair services for defective items. After the warranty period has expired, sellers may offer extended warranty options for an additional 18 months at a cost of $25 per item.

**Exceptions**

* The "Jaxon Home" brand has a 60-day return window, as per a special agreement with Kartway.
* Items sold by "FurnitureHub" are exempt from restocking fees, due to their participation in the Kartway Seller Protection Program.
* The "EcoCycle" line of Furniture items has a 10% restocking fee, as they are made from specialty materials that are difficult to resell.

**Effective Date**
This policy addendum is effective as of January 15, 2023, and supersedes all previous Furniture-related policies.
