# Warranty and Replacement Policy

**Kartway Warranty Policy**

**1. Introduction**
Kartway provides a warranty on products sold through its online marketplace to ensure customer satisfaction and protect against defects. This policy outlines the warranty coverage, terms, and conditions.

**2. Manufacturer versus Kartway Warranty**
Products sold on Kartway are covered by either a manufacturer's warranty or a Kartway warranty. Manufacturer warranties are handled directly by the manufacturer, while Kartway warranties are handled by Kartway's customer operations team. The following categories of products are covered by a Kartway warranty:
	* Electronics: 1-year Kartway warranty
	* Home Goods: 2-year Kartway warranty
	* Fashion: 6-month Kartway warranty
All other products are covered by a manufacturer's warranty, unless explicitly stated otherwise on the product page.

**3. Coverage Periods**
The warranty coverage period varies by product category:
	* Electronics: 1 year from date of delivery
	* Home Goods: 2 years from date of delivery
	* Fashion: 6 months from date of delivery
	* Furniture: 5 years from date of delivery, with the exception of products from manufacturer "Zorvath", which have a 10-year warranty

**4. Voiding Warranty**
The warranty is voided if:
	* The product is damaged due to misuse, neglect, or accident
	* The product is modified or repaired by an unauthorized third party
	* The product is used for commercial purposes (except for products explicitly marked as "commercial-grade")
	* The product is not properly maintained or serviced, as outlined in the product manual

**5. Replace-versus-Repair Decision Rule**
Kartway will replace a defective product if:
	* The product is still under warranty
	* The product is deemed defective due to a manufacturing flaw
	* The repair cost exceeds 50% of the product's original purchase price
Otherwise, Kartway will repair the product.

**6. Turnaround Commitment**
For replacement requests:
	* Kartway will ship a replacement product within 3-5 business days of receiving the defective product
	* The customer will receive a return shipping label via email within 1 business day of submitting a replacement request
For repair requests:
	* Kartway will repair the product within 10-15 business days of receiving the defective product
	* The customer will be notified via email when the repair is complete and the product is shipped back to them
In cases where the manufacturer handles the warranty, Kartway will facilitate communication between the customer and the manufacturer, but the manufacturer's turnaround times and policies will apply.

**7. Exceptions**
The following products are exempt from this warranty policy:
	* Products sold by third-party sellers "Vexar" and "Nexarion"
	* Products marked as "as-is" or "refurbished"
	* Products sold during special promotions, such as "Kartway's Deal of the Day", unless explicitly stated otherwise.
