# Refund Authorisation and Escalation Matrix

**Refund Authorisation Matrix**

1. **Agent Auto-Approval**: Support agents are authorised to approve refunds up to ₹2,000 without requiring additional approval.
2. **Team Lead Approval**: Refund amounts between ₹2,001 and ₹10,000 require approval from a Team Lead.
3. **Operations Manager Approval**: Refund amounts between ₹10,001 and ₹50,000 require approval from an Operations Manager.
4. **Finance Approval**: Refund amounts above ₹50,000 require approval from the Finance department.
5. **Automated System Limitation**: The automated refund system may not auto-approve refunds above ₹2,000, regardless of customer tier or sentiment.
6. **Goodwill Credits**: Goodwill credits may be issued up to a maximum of ₹1,500 per order, and are subject to a separate approval process.
7. **Goodwill Credit Stacking**: Goodwill credits may not be stacked with a full refund on the same order. If a customer is eligible for a full refund, no goodwill credit may be issued.
8. **Exceptions**: The following customers are exempt from the goodwill credit cap: Platinum Tier customers, who may receive up to ₹3,000 in goodwill credits per order, and customers who have been identified as high-value customers by the Customer Success team, who may receive up to ₹2,500 in goodwill credits per order.
9. **Approval Deadlines**: All refund approvals must be completed within 3 business days of the initial refund request. Refund requests that require Finance approval must be escalated to the Finance department within 2 business days.
10. **Audit and Review**: The Refund Authorisation Matrix will be reviewed and updated quarterly by the Customer Operations team, with input from the Finance department.
