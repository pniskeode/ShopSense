# Category Policy Addendum: Home & Kitchen

**Addendum to Customer Operations Policy: Home & Kitchen Category**

1. **Return Window**: For Home & Kitchen category items, the return window is 45 days from the date of delivery, except for orders totaling over $200, which have a 60-day return window. This supersedes the standard 30-day return policy.
2. **Condition Requirements**: Items must be returned in their original condition with all parts, accessories, and instructions included. For furniture and large appliances, a restocking fee of 15% will be applied if the item is not in its original condition, with a minimum fee of $25 and a maximum fee of $100.
3. **Original Packaging**: For Home & Kitchen items, original packaging is mandatory for returns of electronics, such as coffee makers and blenders. For all other items in this category, original packaging is recommended but not required.
4. **Restocking Fees**: A restocking fee of 10% will be applied to all returns of Home & Kitchen items valued over $50, except for orders placed by Kartway Premier members, who are exempt from restocking fees on orders under $150.
5. **Hazardous or Perishable Handling**: The sale of perishable food items is prohibited on the Kartway marketplace. For hazardous materials, such as cleaning supplies, sellers must comply with Kartway's Hazardous Materials Shipping Policy, which requires special packaging and labeling. A handling fee of $12 will be applied to all orders containing hazardous materials.
6. **Warranty Period**: The warranty period for Home & Kitchen items is 2 years from the date of delivery, except for items from the "Kartway Certified" program, which have a 5-year warranty. The warranty period for items from seller "GreenEarth" is 3 years, as per their special agreement with Kartway.
7. **Exceptions**: The following sellers are exempt from the restocking fee for returns: "KitchenDelights" (up to 2 returns per customer per year), "HomeEssentials" (for orders under $75), and "FurnitureFrenzy" (for orders placed during the annual Kartway Summer Sale, which runs from June 15 to July 15).
