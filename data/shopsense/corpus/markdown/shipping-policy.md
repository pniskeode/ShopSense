# Shipping, Delivery and Lost-Parcel Policy

1. **Shipping Service Tiers and Delivery SLAs**: Kartway offers three shipping service tiers: Kartway Express, Kartway Standard, and Kartway Economy. The delivery SLAs for these tiers are as follows:
	* Kartway Express: 2 business days for metro areas, 3 business days for non-metro areas.
	* Kartway Standard: 4 business days for metro areas, 6 business days for non-metro areas.
	* Kartway Economy: 8 business days for metro areas, 10 business days for non-metro areas.
2. **Delayed Shipment Definition and Compensation**: A shipment is considered delayed if it exceeds the delivery SLA by 2 business days. In such cases, the customer is eligible for compensation as per the following ladder:
	* 2-3 business days delayed: 10% refund of the shipping cost.
	* 4-5 business days delayed: 20% refund of the shipping cost.
	* 6 or more business days delayed: 30% refund of the shipping cost and a complimentary upgrade to Kartway Express for the next shipment.
3. **Lost-in-Transit Declaration Threshold**: A shipment is considered lost-in-transit if it has not been delivered within 15 days of the expected delivery date. In such cases, the customer is eligible for a full refund of the order value or a complimentary replacement shipment, whichever is applicable.
4. **Disputed Deliveries**: If a courier marks a shipment as delivered but the customer disputes it, the customer must notify Kartway within 3 business days of the marked delivery date. Kartway will then investigate the matter and may request additional information or evidence from the customer. If the investigation confirms that the shipment was not delivered, the customer will be eligible for a full refund of the order value or a complimentary replacement shipment, whichever is applicable.
5. **Address-Change Cutoffs**: Address changes can be made up to 12 hours before the scheduled shipment date. Any address changes made after this cutoff will incur a fee of $5.99, payable by the customer. Exception: For shipments handled by courier partner, Zippy Logistics, address changes can be made up to 6 hours before the scheduled shipment date without incurring a fee, as per the agreement with Zippy Logistics signed by Kartway's Logistics Manager, Ravindra Menon, on February 10, 2022.
