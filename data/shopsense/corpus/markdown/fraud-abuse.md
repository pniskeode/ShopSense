# Return Fraud and Abuse Prevention Standard

**Kartway Customer Operations Policy**
**Document ID: CO-001**
**Revision: 2.0**
**Effective Date: 2023-02-15**

**Clause 1: Return Abuse Definition**
For the purposes of this policy, "return abuse" refers to a pattern of behavior where a customer repeatedly returns items or requests refunds in excess of a reasonable expectation, as defined in Clause 2.

**Clause 2: Return-Rate Threshold**
A customer's account will be flagged for review if they exceed a return rate of 20% within a 90-day period, or if they have more than 5 returns within a 180-day period. The return rate is calculated by dividing the number of returned items by the total number of orders placed during the specified period.

**Clause 3: Serial Refund Requesters**
Customers who have been flagged for return abuse will be subject to the following rules:
3.1. If a customer has 2 or more returns within a 30-day period, they will be limited to a maximum of 1 return per 30-day period for the next 120 days.
3.2. If a customer has 3 or more returns within a 60-day period, their account will be suspended for 30 days, and they will be required to contact Customer Support to have their account reinstated.

**Clause 4: Confidentiality of Flagged Accounts**
Support assistants are prohibited from disclosing to a customer that their account has been flagged for return abuse. In the event a customer inquires about their return history or account status, the assistant will respond with a generic statement, such as "We're reviewing your account to ensure compliance with our policies."

**Clause 5: Escalation Route for Suspected Fraud**
If a support assistant suspects that a customer is engaging in fraudulent activity, such as returning items that were not purchased from Kartway or attempting to obtain refunds for items that were not defective, they will escalate the issue to the Fraud Review Team (FRT) via the internal ticketing system. The FRT will review the case and take appropriate action, which may include account suspension or termination. The FRT will also notify the Customer Operations Manager, Zara Saeed, who will review the case and provide guidance on next steps.

**Clause 6: Exception for VIP Customers**
The rules outlined in Clause 3 do not apply to customers who have achieved VIP status, as defined by the Kartway Loyalty Program. VIP customers will be subject to a separate set of rules and guidelines, which will be outlined in a separate policy document.

**Clause 7: Review and Revision**
This policy will be reviewed and revised as necessary, but at least annually. Changes to this policy will be approved by the Customer Operations Manager, Zara Saeed, and communicated to all support assistants via the internal knowledge base.
