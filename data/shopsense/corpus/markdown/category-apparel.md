# Category Policy Addendum: Apparel

**Addendum to Kartway Customer Operations Policy: Apparel Category**

1.1. **Return Window**: For Apparel items, the return window is 45 days from the date of delivery, exceeding the standard 30-day window to accommodate sizing and fit issues.

1.2. **Condition Requirements**: Apparel items must be returned in their original condition with all tags attached. Specifically, shoes must have no more than 5 miles of wear, as determined by the sole's tread depth, and clothing must have no signs of alteration, with a maximum of 2% fabric fading, as measured by the Kartway Fabric Fading Index.

1.3. **Original Packaging**: For Apparel items, original packaging is mandatory for returns and exchanges, except for items from the "Kartway Essentials" brand, where packaging is not required for returns.

1.4. **Restocking Fees**: A restocking fee of 12% of the item's price will be applied to all Apparel returns, except for items priced above $120, where the fee is capped at $15.

1.5. **Hazardous or Perishable Handling**: Apparel items containing hazardous materials, such as those with azo dyes, must be handled and returned in accordance with Kartway's Hazardous Materials Policy. Perishable Apparel items, such as those with built-in insect repellent, must be returned within 20 days of delivery.

1.6. **Warranty Period**: The warranty period for Apparel items is 2 years from the date of delivery, with the exception of items from the "Protean Athletics" brand, which carry a 5-year warranty. During this period, customers are eligible for free repairs or replacements for defects in materials and workmanship, with a maximum of 3 repairs per item.

1.7. **Exceptions**: The above policies do not apply to custom or personalized Apparel items, such as those with embroidery or monograms, which are subject to the terms and conditions outlined in the Kartway Custom Orders Policy. Specifically, custom orders from designer "Luna Nightingale" are exempt from restocking fees and have a 60-day return window.

By adhering to these category-specific guidelines, support agents can ensure a consistent and satisfactory experience for Kartway customers purchasing Apparel items.
