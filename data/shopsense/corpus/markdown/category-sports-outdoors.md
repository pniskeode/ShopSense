# Category Policy Addendum: Sports & Outdoors

**Kartway Sports & Outdoors Return and Warranty Policy Addendum**

1. **Return Window**: For Sports & Outdoors items, the return window is 45 days from the date of delivery, exceeding the standard 30-day window to accommodate seasonal purchasing patterns.
2. **Condition Requirements**: All returned Sports & Outdoors items must be in their original condition with no signs of use, except for items valued over $200, which can be returned with minor signs of use, provided they are still in functional condition. For items like bicycles and skateboards, a 10% restocking fee will be applied if the item shows any signs of outdoor use.
3. **Original Packaging**: For certain high-value items (over $500), such as golf clubs and high-end fitness equipment, original packaging is mandatory for returns. This includes all original accessories, manuals, and packaging materials. For items under $500, original packaging is recommended but not required.
4. **Restocking Fees**: A restocking fee of 15% will be applied to all returned Sports & Outdoors items valued over $100, except for items from the "Kartway Certified" program, which are exempt from restocking fees. This fee will be waived if the item is defective or if the return is due to an error on Kartway's part.
5. **Hazardous or Perishable Handling**: For hazardous materials like batteries and perishable items like nutrition supplements, special handling procedures must be followed. Sellers must disclose the hazardous nature of their items and provide proper packaging and shipping instructions. Buyers must return these items within 20 days and are responsible for ensuring they are packaged and shipped according to the seller's instructions.
6. **Warranty Period**: The warranty period for Sports & Outdoors items is 2 years from the date of purchase, except for items from the brands "Trailblazer" and "FitnessPro", which have a 5-year warranty period. For items like treadmills and elliptical machines, the warranty period is 3 years, with the option to extend to 5 years for an additional $50 fee.

Exceptions to this policy include:

- Items purchased during the annual "Kartway Summer Sale" (occurring between June 15th and July 15th), which have a return window of 60 days.
- Purchases made by "Kartway Pro" members, who are eligible for a 90-day return window and waived restocking fees on all Sports & Outdoors items.
