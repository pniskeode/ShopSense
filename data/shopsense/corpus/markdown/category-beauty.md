# Category Policy Addendum: Beauty

**Kartway Customer Operations Policy Handbook: Beauty Category Addendum**

**1. Return Window**
For Beauty category items, the return window is 45 days from the date of delivery, except for items marked as "Final Sale" by the seller, in which case returns are not accepted.

**2. Condition Requirements**
All returned Beauty items must be in their original condition with 75% or more of the product remaining, except for items with a net weight of 120ml or less, which must be at least 90% full.

**3. Original Packaging**
Original packaging is mandatory for returns of Beauty items with a value of $25 or more, with the exception of items from the "Kartway Luxe" collection, which can be returned without original packaging.

**4. Restocking Fees**
A restocking fee of 18% will be applied to all returned Beauty items valued at $50 or more, except for items from the "Natural Bliss" brand, which will incur a restocking fee of 12%.

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling**
Beauty items containing hazardous materials, such as aerosols or flammable liquids, must be handled and shipped in accordance with Kartway's Hazardous Materials Policy. Perishable Beauty items, such as skincare products with a shelf life of less than 24 months, must be returned within 14 days of delivery.

**6. Warranty Period**
The warranty period for Beauty items is 2 years from the date of purchase, except for electrical beauty devices, which have a warranty period of 5 years. Items from the "Glowing Skin" brand have a warranty period of 3 years.

**7. Special Exceptions**
The following sellers are exempt from the 45-day return window: Bella Beauty Co. (60-day return window) and Pure Perfection Cosmetics (30-day return window). Additionally, items from the "Kartway Kouture" collection are not eligible for returns, except in cases of defect or damage, in which case a replacement or refund will be provided within 10 business days of receipt of the return request.
